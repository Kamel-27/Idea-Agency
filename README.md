# Idea Agency

Website and lead generation system for **Idea Agency**, a digital marketing and web agency in Cairo. Also our DEPI "Advanced Marketing Automation with AI" graduation project.

An SME owner clicks our ad, lands on a page and requests a free marketing diagnostic. An automation scores the lead with AI, saves it in a CRM, replies by email and WhatsApp, follows up, and a dashboard shows everything.
