# 02 Business, audience and offer

## The agency

**IDEA Marketing Agency**: a hybrid agency for Egyptian SMEs that combines strategy with execution and measurable performance.

- Proposed promise: **"From Marketing Strategy to Measurable Action."** (a proposed message, not a claim of exclusivity or guaranteed results)
- Positioning statement (from the strategy file): For Egyptian small and medium-sized businesses seeking sustainable growth, IDEA is a hybrid marketing partner that combines strategic planning with tailored execution and measurable performance management, helping businesses make informed marketing decisions through a clear, collaborative and accountable process.
- Not medical. The agency and project are not health-related.

## Target audience

**Primary target:** growth-stage SME owners in Greater Cairo who already spend on Facebook/Instagram (boosting posts, paying a freelancer) but do not know what is working.

**Not our target:** micro businesses with no marketing budget, and large companies with in-house marketing teams.

### Persona 1 (primary): Mariam, growth-seeking owner
- Owns and decides for a business of roughly 5 to 49 employees
- Goal: more sales
- Pains: spends on marketing without clarity on return, fragmented vendors and freelancers
- Trigger: marketing spend without clarity. Objection: "How will I know it works?"
- Message: "Know what to improve before spending more."
- Offer for her: Marketing Diagnostic + Growth Plan

### Persona 2: Ahmed, performance-focused manager
- Marketing manager in a more structured SME
- Goal: better ROI, aligned channels, clear reporting
- Trigger: leadership asks for improvement. Objection: "What do you add beyond my team?"
- Message: "Align channels, teams and measurable objectives."
- Offer: Integrated Strategy + Reporting

### Persona 3: Mohamed, digital transition owner (later, not the launch focus)
- Traditional/offline business, competitors gaining online visibility
- Objection: "Do I need a full agency?"
- Offer: Starter Digital Setup

These personas are proto-personas (hypotheses). The team validates them with 3 short calls with real SME owners.

## Services (3-step offer ladder)

| Tier | Service | What it includes | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry (lead magnet) | **Free SME Marketing Diagnostic** | 20-minute call + 1-page action plan: how the business gets customers today, what is working, the 3 biggest leaks, next 30 days | Free |
| Core | **Strategy Blueprint** (one-time project) | Research, target audience, messaging, channel plan, KPIs, 90-day roadmap | Proposed, to test |
| Retainer | **Execution + optimization** (monthly) | Content, paid ads, SEO, digital operations, monthly performance review and improvements. Ad spend paid separately | Proposed, to test |

Only sell what the team can actually deliver.

## Example client journey

Sara owns a clothing store in Nasr City with 8 employees. She spends about 10,000 EGP a month boosting Instagram posts but sales are not growing.

1. **Free diagnostic.** She sees our ad, fills the form, instantly gets a WhatsApp message and email: "Hi Sara, thanks! You said your Instagram ads are not bringing sales. We will call you within 2 hours." On the call we review her page and ads and send a 1-page plan: she boosts every post instead of running 2 proper ads to a clear offer; there is no WhatsApp button on her page so interested people get lost; 3 actions for the next 30 days.
2. **Strategy Blueprint.** She wants the full version: audience, messages, platforms, budget split, 90-day plan.
3. **Retainer.** She asks us to run it: content, ads, tracking, monthly report.

The graduation project demonstrates step 1 working end to end.

Review themes customers care about: responsiveness and communication, business-specific advice, seeing business outcomes, clear scope, smooth handover from strategy to execution. Our service design answers these: named account owner, response time promise, written scope, baseline KPIs and reporting.

## SWOT (short)

- Strengths: integrated strategy + execution, configurable tiers, chance to standardize transparent delivery
- Weaknesses: new brand, no case studies yet, budget and capacity not fixed
- Opportunities: huge SME base with wide digital access, partnerships, multiple entry points by maturity
- Threats: established agencies, free audit alternatives, price sensitivity, weak ROI attribution

Market context (strategy file, sourced): about 3.7m economic establishments in Egypt (2017 census, 91.7% micro, OECD 2025); 98.2m internet users and 51.6m social media identities (DataReportal Digital 2026). Do not multiply establishments by a price to fake a market size.
