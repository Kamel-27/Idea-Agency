# 01 Project brief

## The assignment

DEPI Advanced Marketing Automation with AI, final team capstone. We chose **Elite Project 2: Automated Lead Generation Engine with CRM and Reporting** (it falls back to Standard Project 2, "Service Business Lead Generation and CRM Follow-Up System", if the elite demo is not ready).

**Elite Project 2, expected outcomes**
- Full-funnel lead generation architecture
- Landing page or form with qualification logic and source attribution
- UTM and event tracking plan
- CRM database with lifecycle stages and lead status rules
- Automation workflow for lead capture, AI response, team notification, and status update
- Looker Studio or Sheets dashboard with weekly reporting logic

**Advanced requirement:** demonstrate at least one live or simulated workflow, show the system architecture, test multiple scenarios, and explain the strategic and technical decisions.

**Evaluated on:** system cohesion, lead quality logic, attribution accuracy, dashboard usefulness, automation quality, follow-up personalization, and whether the data supports real decisions.

**Standard Project 2 adds:** service offer, audience segmentation and objection map; three-step email/WhatsApp/Messenger follow-up sequence; CRM with lead source, status, priority and next action.

Suggested tools: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google Trends, Meta Ads Library, Canva, CapCut, Figma, Carrd, EasyOrders, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Google Sheets, Make.com, n8n, Airtable, Gmail, WhatsApp Business API simulation, Messenger/ManyChat simulation.

## Documentation structure the guidelines require

1. Project planning and management (objectives, success criteria, Gantt, tools, roles, risks)
2. Business model and strategic review (Business Model Canvas, SWOT, market, AI use cases, why this project)
3. Requirements gathering (market/audience/competitor data, personas, CRM data fields, functional and non-functional requirements)
4. System analysis and design (strategy, funnel, content, tracking, workflow maps, CRM structure, AI prompt logic, testing scenarios)
5. Implementation (deliverables, working workflows, sample data, screenshots)
6. Final presentation, testing and reports (final report, testing report, deck, demo, Q&A)
