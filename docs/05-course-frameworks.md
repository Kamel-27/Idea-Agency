# 05 Course frameworks (DEPI Sessions 1 to 20)

Summary of what each lecture teaches. Graders look for these frameworks by name, so use the exact names in documents and code comments where relevant. Sessions 12 and 21+ were not available when this was written; Make.com, n8n, CRM tools and Looker Studio are taught in later lectures.

Cross-session patterns graders expect: one brand carried through everything; Egyptian context (EGP, COD, WhatsApp); "AI drafts, human verifies" with sources; prompts written as Role + Task + Constraints + Output format.

## Session 1: Marketing Logic and Customer Psychology
- The Four Questions: WHO is the customer, WHAT problem, HOW the offer solves it, WHY us
- Marketing (strategy) vs Branding (perception) vs Advertising (distribution). Process: Understand, Position, Communicate, Convert
- Emotional drivers (status, belonging, fear, identity) vs rational justifiers (price, quality, convenience, necessity)
- Cialdini biases: scarcity, social proof, reciprocity, anchoring, authority, liking
- 5-stage Customer Decision Journey: Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Purchase, Post-purchase (feel / do / what you do)
- 7Cs: Customer, Cost, Convenience, Communication, Content, Community, Conversion
- Prompt pattern Role, Context, Structure. "AI is a confident liar": use grounding, steel-man ideas with 3 failure reasons and sources

## Session 2: Brand Positioning, Identity and Value Proposition
- Brand identity (sender) vs brand image (receiver)
- 6 elements of identity: name, visual system, voice and tone, story, values, personality
- Positioning formula: "For [target] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [benefit] because [reason to believe]". Difference test: could a competitor say it and be believed?
- 6 messaging clarity principles; template "We help [target] get [benefit] without [pain]"
- 12 brand archetypes (pick 1 primary + 1 secondary)
- Value Proposition Canvas: jobs, pains, gains vs products, pain relievers, gain creators. "Pain relievers column IS your marketing copy." Validate with 3 real customers

## Session 3: Market Research, Persona and Competitor Analysis
- TAM / SAM / SOM. Egyptian sources: CAPMAS, Statista, We Are Social, CBE
- Persona: 6 elements: Identity, Goals, Pain points, Objections, Channels, Influence. Validate against real profiles
- Competitors: direct + indirect; Meta Ad Library long-running ads (30+ days) as swipe file; Google Maps 1-star review mining
- Positioning Gap = customer pain + competitor failure + our credible solution. Sentence: "We own [gap] because [reason], verified by [source]"

## Session 4: The Strategy Blueprint
- SMART goals, PESTEL then SWOT with a "so what?" paragraph
- STP: 4 segmentation lenses, one primary target, one named not-target, 5-question test
- 4Ps + People, Process, Physical evidence. Pricing lenses: cost, competition, value
- Budget split 60% acquisition / 25% retention / 15% testing; auto-pause at 3x target CPA for 3 days
- AARRR funnel; OKRs: "Objective [verb][noun][by when]; KR1 to 3 [number, metric, date]", one owner per KR
- 1-Page Strategy Blueprint: Position, Target, Offer, Channels, Objective, Key Results, Risks
- Campaign Planning Template: Objective, Audience, Offer, Channels, Budget, Timeline, Success metric (e.g. CPL under 35 EGP)

## Session 5: Persuasive Copywriting
- Frameworks: PAS (ads, low trust), AIDA (long captions, emails), BAB (transformations), HVPC Hook Value Proof CTA (short form), SLO, HCSCT
- 6 hook patterns: Curiosity Gap, Negation, Number, Direct Address, Bold Claim, Reframe (max 8 words)
- 3-step AI edit loop: critique, cut 30%, cut 20%
- Strong CTA: one ask, direct verb, low friction, a reason ("Comment AUDIT and we DM the checklist")

## Session 6: Brand Voice and Content Workflow
- Voice = DNA, tone = outfit. 6-axis voice grid. 1-page voice doc: tone words, sentence rules, words to use/avoid, 2 example captions
- 4 content pillars: Educate 50, Convert 25, Engage 15, Inspire 10. CTA by pillar: save, DM, comment, share
- Caption formula Hook, Promise, Proof, CTA. Batch system: Plan, Write, Shoot, Schedule
- Workflow KPIs include Convert-post DM-to-close rate

## Session 7: Storytelling Frameworks
- Freytag, Three-Act, Pixar Story Spine, Hero's Journey (5 stages), ABT
- StoryBrand BrandScript: Character, Problem (external/internal/philosophical), Guide (empathy + authority), Plan (3 steps), CTA (direct + transitional), Success, Failure
- Brand-as-hero trap: the customer is the hero, the brand is the guide
- 3-story brand pack: origin, customer transformation, mission. Email opening: subject + 4-line open

## Session 8: Design Principles and Visual Identity
- 6 principles: hierarchy, contrast, alignment, repetition, white space, proximity
- 60-30-10 color rule; 5-color palette (primary, secondary, neutral light, neutral dark, accent); WCAG 4.5:1 (3:1 large text)
- 2-font rule; "Bilingual Egypt" pair Cairo + Inter; type scale H1 32 to 44, H2 20 to 28, body 14 to 16
- 3 logo versions: full color, mark only, monochrome; clear space and minimum size rules
- Layout templates: quote card, 3-step carousel, big number stat, tip list, before/after, hero + body
- AI layout prompt: Role / Task / Brand / Constraints / Output

## Session 9: AI Image Generation and Compact Brand Kit
- 6-part prompt: Subject, Style, Lighting, Composition, Camera, Detail. Force Egyptian context. Negative prompts
- 10-prompt library per brand; 5-image consistency test; never ship v1; add text in Canva, never in AI
- 5-page brand kit: logo, colors, typography, layout templates (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 1.91:1), voice + image rules
- 5 master Canva templates

## Session 10: Video Strategy and Scripts
- 3-stage video funnel: Awareness 15 to 30s no CTA, Consideration 60 to 90s soft CTA, Conversion 30 to 60s hard CTA
- Audience temperature cold / warm / hot. Hook deadlines: TikTok 1.5s, Reels 2s, LinkedIn 5s
- HMPC formula: Hook 1.5s (max 8 words), Message, Proof, CTA (one action)
- Six hook patterns: Cold Open, Bold Stat, Negation, Surprising Question, Money Reveal, Pattern Break
- Retention under 50% = bad hook, over 70% = strong

## Session 11: Video Production and Storyboarding
- 80% rule (quality decided before recording); pre-production checklist; shot list (# / scene / framing / action / audio / duration, 8 to 12 shots)
- Framing types, six camera moves, rule of thirds, window light, audio checklist
- AI video: image-to-video beats text-to-video; film authenticity, generate atmosphere
- Phone settings 1080p 30fps; folder structure and file naming

## Session 13: Ecommerce and Performance Marketing
- Brand vs performance; Egypt reality: ~80% COD, 50%+ via WhatsApp/DM
- KPI formulas: CTR = clicks / impressions; CPC = spend / clicks; CPA = spend / conversions; ROAS = revenue / spend (3x min); LTV:CAC 3:1
- Diagnose backwards from the worst metric. The 3-leg stool: Offer + Traffic + Conversion
- Single-variable test rule: one change per week, measure 7 days, kill after 14

## Session 14: Offer Design and Conversion Psychology
- Product-Market-Message alignment. Value stack: stack components, add values, reveal price (value 3x to 8x price)
- Pre-empt 4 objections: too expensive, do not trust, will it work for me, what if I am wrong
- 6 risk reversals; 6 psychological triggers (use about 3 per offer)
- Offer by temperature: cold = free guide/consultation, warm = bundle/proof, hot = urgency/personal nudge
- 3-tier offer ladder. Services example: free brand audit, then a 5,000 EGP project, then a 25,000 EGP monthly retainer

## Session 15: Tracking, Pixels and Events
- 4 layers: browser, tag manager, platform, dashboard. Client vs server side (CAPI)
- Track the 5 to 7 events that matter. Meta standard events (exact casing): PageView, ViewContent, Lead, CompleteRegistration, Contact, etc. GA4 snake_case: generate_lead, sign_up
- Cross-platform event map from one GTM trigger. Required parameters: value, currency=EGP
- Consent banner essentials; Egypt PDPL. AEM, Event Match Quality 7+
- Deliverable: 1-page tracking plan with 6 sections (brand + URL, funnel actions, event taxonomy, platforms, privacy stance, validation checklist) + GTM Preview and Test Events screenshots

## Session 16: Traffic, UTM and Attribution
- 5 UTM parameters; 3 rules: lowercase, no spaces (underscores), stick to the dictionary. Max 5 sources, 4 mediums
- Campaign template goal_audience_date
- Attribution models: first, last, linear, time-decay, position-based, data-driven (needs ~600 conversions/month, otherwise last-click)
- Budget 70/20/10 proven / emerging / experimental; stage split; scale 20% a week
- Remarketing audience minimums: Meta 1,000+, Google 100+

## Session 17: Landing Page Strategy
- Single-goal principle; paid in = LP, organic in = website
- 5 above-the-fold elements; 5-second headline test; 4 headline formulas; message match
- 6 trust elements for Egyptian LPs; 3-field form (name + phone + city); 6 CTA rules and placements
- 4-section anatomy; mobile-first wireframes 390x844 and 1440x900; hero under 200KB; PageSpeed 70+
- 10-point LP audit, 0 to 2 each

## Session 18: Building Landing Pages
- 7 sections: Hero, Social proof, Problem, Solution, Features, Testimonials, Closing CTA
- Headline "[Outcome] for [Audience] [Differentiator]"; single CTA repeated 3 to 5 times, sticky on mobile
- Builders: Carrd default, Tilda, Webflow, Framer; EasyOrders for Egypt ecommerce
- Mobile 44px taps, 16px text; speed targets; Arabic RTL rules
- WhatsApp link wa.me/20XXXXXXXXX?text=...
- 4 events every LP must fire; verify a test lead within 24h before paid traffic
- Deliverables: polished LP (PageSpeed 80+), Conversion Baseline doc

## Session 19: Funnel Architecture
- AARRR with one KPI per letter: "five numbers, one dashboard, weekly review"
- Conversion math: stage rates multiply
- Lead-gen funnel: Ad, Landing page (lead magnet, single CTA), Form, Thank-you + delivery, Nurture (5 to 10 touches over 14 days)
- 6 lead magnets incl. 30-minute consult call and calculator/assessment
- Complete funnel map, 6 components: sources, pages, conversion events, retargeting, follow-up, KPI per node (Miro)
- Funnel brief (250 words): "My funnel takes [source] to [page] to [conversion]. The biggest leak is [X]. The first fix is [Y]."

## Session 20: Multi-Source Retargeting and Follow-Up
- Platform jobs: awareness (TikTok/Meta, no CTA), consideration (Meta retargeting, soft CTA), conversion (Google Search + hot retargeting, hard CTA), retention (email + WhatsApp)
- Budget 60/30/10 awareness / retargeting / testing
- Cold / warm / hot routing: "why this exists" / "why this is for you" / "why now"
- Unified LP if budget under 10K EGP/month, with UTMs for per-source conversion
- 4 retargeting tiers with frequency caps
- Email = nurture, WhatsApp = closing. 5-email sequence (Day 0, 2, 4, 7, 14). WhatsApp 4 triggers. WhatsApp opt-in mandatory
- Tag leads by source (Mailchimp tags, WhatsApp labels Lead-Meta / Lead-Google)
